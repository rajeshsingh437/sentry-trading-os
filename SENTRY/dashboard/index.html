Mission Principles

The discipline behind every trade
I

Longevity First

Survive long enough for compounding to work. Never break risk parameters or chase desperate trades.

II

Process Over Result

Execution discipline matters more than short-term outcomes.

III

Detach From Single Trades

Don't get tied to one P&L or outcome. Think in terms of the bigger journey.

IV

Focus on Horizons

Review weekly at minimum, ideally monthly. Patterns only become trends over time.

Why This Tab Exists

The gap between knowing and doing

Your Rule Engine and Cool-Down system (Rulebook tab) already act as circuit breakers — they stop you mechanically. This tab targets the layer before that: what you tell yourself to do in the exact moment an urge shows up, so you never have to rely on willpower alone. Willpower fails under stress; pre-committed responses hold up because the decision was already made when you were calm.

If-Then Implementation Intentions

—

Pre-commit your response to each known trigger. When the trigger happens, you don't decide — you just run the response. Tick a rule when you actually follow it live; the streak underneath shows how consistently you're keeping your own word.

60-Second Reset

Box breathing · run before you touch the order window

The urge to revenge-trade or chase FOMO is a physiological spike, not a thought — this interrupts it. Use it the moment you notice the urge, before you place the order, not after.
